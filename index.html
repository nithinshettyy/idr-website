About IDR

An institute built by the industry,
for the industry

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute dedicated to building capability in digital, cyber and technology risk. We serve regulated, high-consequence environments — from financial services to critical national infrastructure — where the stakes demand more than compliance checklists.

Our model bridges the gap between academic insight and practitioner reality. Through a formal partnership with leading UK universities, IDR programmes are underpinned by research depth while remaining tightly coupled to live industry projects, emerging threat landscapes, and real employer needs.

Our Model

Three pillars. One integrated model.

IDR's delivery model combines structured training, applied innovation, and advisory deployment — turning talent into practitioners and problems into solutions.

01

IDR Academy

Structured bootcamps, apprenticeships and CPD programmes for students and professionals entering or advancing in digital and cyber risk roles. Curriculum co-designed with employers and academic partners.

Bootcamps Apprenticeships CPD Certifications
02

Innovation & Incubation

Developing novel IP, next-generation risk models and AI governance frameworks. IDR's incubation arm partners with startups and research teams to commercialise applied digital risk innovation.

AI Governance IP Development Risk Models R&D
03

Advisory Services

Helping regulated organisations implement, audit and mature risk frameworks — including NIST CSF, ISO 27001, NIS2, DORA, and AI governance standards. Delivered by practitioner-led teams.

NIST CSF ISO 27001 NIS2 DORA
