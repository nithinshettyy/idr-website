# Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) – Brand & Homepage Assignment

This repository contains my submission for the **Institute of Digital Risk (IDR)** design assignment.  
The task was to create a **simple brand identity and responsive homepage** for an industry-led training institute focused on **digital risk, cyber risk and AI governance**.

## 🎨 Logo Design

The logo uses a **geometric cube structure** to represent the layered and structured nature of digital risk management.  
The **orange colour** symbolizes alertness, innovation and proactive risk response, while **black and white** provide strong contrast and a professional technology-focused identity.  
The typography combines a **modern serif and clean sans-serif style**, balancing academic credibility with a contemporary digital feel.  
The cube also reflects the institute’s focus on **structured frameworks, resilience and multi-dimensional risk analysis**.
